# 📌 Project Overview

**Genesis Diagnostic** is a professional website developed for a medical diagnostic laboratory located in Hafeezpet, Hyderabad, Telangana.

The website is designed to provide customers with a clear, trustworthy, and easy-to-navigate digital experience while presenting the laboratory's services, testing categories, business information, and contact details.

The project focuses on:

- Professional medical branding
- Clear presentation of diagnostic services
- Advanced genetic testing information
- Test and pricing information
- Responsive design
- Mobile-first usability
- Direct phone-based customer contact
- Accurate business information
- Search-engine-friendly structure
- Clean and maintainable React architecture

The website is designed as a **customer-facing business website** rather than an online medical consultation or patient-management system.

# 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of the website is to establish a professional online presence for Genesis Diagnostic and make important laboratory information easily accessible to customers.

The website allows visitors to:

1. Understand the laboratory and its services
2. Explore diagnostic testing categories
3. View available tests and pricing information
4. Learn about advanced genetic testing
5. Understand the laboratory's offerings
6. Learn about the partnered network
7. Access business contact information
8. Directly call the laboratory
9. Find the laboratory location
10. Access the website from mobile, tablet, and desktop devices

---

# 💡 Business Problem

A diagnostic laboratory needs to communicate important information clearly and professionally.

Customers commonly need to know:

- What services are available?
- What tests are offered?
- How much do tests cost?
- Where is the laboratory located?
- How can the laboratory be contacted?
- Are advanced testing options available?
- Is home sample collection available?

A traditional offline communication model can make this information difficult to access.

The Genesis Diagnostic website provides a centralized digital platform for this information.
